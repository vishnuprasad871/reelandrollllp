# Reel & Roll Photography — Admin Guide

## Overview

The Admin Panel lets you manage the photo gallery on your website. You can upload new images, organise them by category, and remove images you no longer want to display.

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Panel

Open your browser and go to:

```
https://reelandroll.com/admin/
```

---

## 2. Logging In

Enter your credentials on the login screen:

| Field    | Value               |

| Password | `admin123`          |

Click **Sign In**. You will be taken to the Gallery Manager dashboard.

> **Keep your password safe.** Do not share your login credentials with anyone.

---

## 3. Uploading a New Photo

1. On the dashboard, locate the **"Upload New Image"** section at the top.
2. Click the upload area (or drag and drop your image file onto it).
3. A preview of the selected image will appear.
4. Fill in the details:

   | Field        | Description |
   |--------------|-------------|
   | **Category** | Choose the category that best fits the photo: Wedding, Portrait, Event, or Landscape |
   | **Alt Text** | A short description of the image (e.g. "Wedding ceremony at sunset") |

5. Click **Upload Image**.
6. A success message will appear and the image will be added to your gallery automatically.

**Supported formats:** JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP  
**Maximum file size:** 5 MB

---

## 4. Managing Existing Photos

Below the upload section you will see all your gallery images in a grid.

### Filtering by Category

Use the tab buttons to view images by category:
- **All** — shows every image
- **Wedding** / **Portrait** / **Event** / **Landscape** — filters to that category

### Editing an Image

1. Click the **Edit** button on any image card.
2. An edit window will open where you can change the **Category** or **Alt Text**.
3. Click **Save Changes** to apply.

### Deleting an Image

1. Click the **Delete** button on the image card.
2. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

> ⚠️ **Deleted images cannot be recovered.** Please be sure before deleting.

---

## 5. Logging Out

Click the **Logout** button in the top-right corner of the dashboard to securely end your session.

---
