Our Workflow

Eight-Step Excellence

Every project follows our proven process designed for quality, communication, and client satisfaction

01

Initial Consultation

📅 1-2 Hours

We begin with an in-depth conversation to understand your vision, style preferences, event details, and any special requirements. This session helps us align on creative direction, discuss technical specifications, and answer all your questions.

Vision Discovery Budget Discussion Timeline Planning
02

Custom Proposal & Quote

📅 2-3 Days

Based on our discussion, we create a tailored proposal including package details, deliverables, and timeline. This proposal is customized to your specific needs and ensures complete clarity before moving forward.

Package Options Clear Timeline Deliverables List
03

Creative Planning & Mood Board

📅 5-7 Days

Once you approve the proposal, we begin creative development. Our team creates mood boards, discusses styling, location scouting (if applicable), and refines the creative direction to ensure we're perfectly aligned with your aesthetic.

Mood Board Creation Location Scouting Styling Consultation
04

Agreement & Booking Confirmation

📅 1 Day

We formalize the engagement with a service agreement outlining all terms. A deposits (25-50%) is required to secure your date and reserve our team. The remaining balance is due before delivery.

Service Agreement Date Reservation Deposit Required
05

Pre-Production Setup

📅 7-14 Days Before

Our production team prepares everything meticulously. This includes equipment checks, lighting setup, location final walkthrough, team briefing, and contingency planning. We ensure all technical aspects are perfected before the shoot day.

Equipment Prep Lighting Design Backup Plans
06

The Shoot Day

📅 Varies by Type

Our team arrives fully prepared and ready to create magic. With professional direction, world-class technical execution, and artistic vision, we capture every precious moment. We maintain communication and ensure your complete satisfaction during the session.

Professional Artistry Technical Excellence Real-time Direction
07

Post-Production & Editing

📅 5-10 Days

Our retouching specialists begin the meticulous editing process immediately. Each image is carefully color-graded, retouched for perfection, and refined to match your brand aesthetic. A first draft is delivered for your review and feedback.

Color Grading Professional Retouching Client Review Draft
08

Final Delivery

📅 3-5 Days After Review

After your feedback and final revisions, we deliver the complete gallery of high-resolution images via secure cloud link or your preferred method. Final payment is due, and you receive all files in multiple formats ready for any use.

High-Res Files Multiple Formats Secure Delivery

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the delivery?

All packages include professionally edited, high-resolution images delivered via secure cloud link. Physical prints, albums, and video compilations are available as add-ons.

Can I request revisions?

Absolutely! We include revision rounds in our standard process. We'll refine the gallery based on your feedback to ensure complete satisfaction.

What happens if the shoot date needs to be postponed?

We offer flexible rescheduling options. If you need to reschedule, we can usually accommodate within reason, though availability is subject to our calendar.

Do you offer rush delivery?

Yes! Express delivery (3-5 days) is available for an additional fee. Contact us for rush project inquiries and availability.

Can I use the images commercially?

Yes! All our packages include full licensing rights for personal and commercial use (excluding resale of the images themselves).

What if weather affects an outdoor shoot?

We always have backup plans. For weather-dependent shoots, we can reschedule or move to an alternative location with proper notice.
